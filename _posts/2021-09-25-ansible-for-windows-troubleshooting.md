Managing Windows machines using Ansible is pretty straightforward and simple as in the document. You need to [configure and enable WinR](https://www.techbeatly.com/2020/12/configure-your-windows-host-to-manage-by-ansible.html) M on your Windows machine and then [open WinRM ports 5985 and 5986](https://www.techbeatly.com/2021/09/how-to-open-winrm-ports-in-the-windows-firewall.html) (HTTPS) in the Windows Firewall (and also in the network firewall if any).
