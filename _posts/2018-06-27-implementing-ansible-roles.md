In a production environment, we will have hundreds and thousands of different types machines to handle. Hence the type of operations and implementation methods will be different from one to another. You can add different tasks or handlers for each type of operation or host, but managing such a playbook is not an easy job. By using [Ansible Roles](https://docs.ansible.com/ansible/2.5/user_guide/playbooks_reuse_roles.html) ﻿ ﻿, you can organize your playbook into smaller and separate playbooks.
