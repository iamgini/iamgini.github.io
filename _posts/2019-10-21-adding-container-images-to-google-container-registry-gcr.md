<figcaption><em>Image : cloud.google.com</em></figcaption> [Google Container Registry](https://cloud.google.com/container-registry/) (GCR) is a service in [Google Cloud Platform](https://cloud.google.com) (GCP) to manage your own docker container repository. This is fully managed service and you can store your custom container images as well as common images from other image repositories. Also, you can implement fine-grained access control on your private container registry using GCR.
