[Ansible](https://www.techbeatly.com/ansible-course) Automation Controller streamlines IT tasks through automation. A key component of this power lies in [workflows](https://docs.ansible.com/automation-controller/latest/html/userguide/workflow_templates.html) . These workflows allow you to orchestrate a sequence of disparate job templates, essentially chaining them together for intricate deployments. Workflows even boast approval nodes, enabling users to pause the process and grant permission before continuing. But what if your scenario demands more nuanced control over these approvals?
