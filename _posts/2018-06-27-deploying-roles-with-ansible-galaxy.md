So you have learned about [Ansible Roles](https://www.techbeatly.com/2018/06/ansible-part-11-implementing-roles.html/) and the importance of using roles. [Ansible Galaxy](https://galaxy.ansible.com/) is a public library where you can find thousands of roles written by other ansible users and administrators. Ansible Galaxy is arranged with a searchable database where you can easily find suitable role which you can re-use in your ansible environment.
